# Autonomous AP Business Case & Cash-Risk Check

A single-page app in one file (`index.html`) with no dependencies. It's the companion tool for an edition of the LinkedIn newsletter **AI in Finance, Done Right**: **"1.5 Million Non-PO Invoices, 6,000 Properties, and 60,000 Hours Saved: How Real Estate Giant HSB Put Accounts Payable on Autopilot"**.

## Features
- **Tab 1, Business case:** Enter your invoices a year, minutes per invoice today, minutes per exception invoice with AI, loaded AP cost, annual spend processed and an expected net cash-exposure rate. Set a no-touch rate yourself or apply the suggested one for your volume (38% under 20,000 invoices, 63% up to 500,000, 72% above that, from the edition's benchmarks). See hours back, the value of that time, expected cash exposure and the net annual case.
- **Tab 2, Controls check:** Six controls to have in place before switching Autopilot on. You get a readiness dial, the biggest gaps and a business case you can copy.

The demo figures are made up. HSB results are as reported by HSB and its vendor; the 0.05% exposure default comes from a separate 2026 benchmark. Everything runs in the browser and nothing is uploaded.
