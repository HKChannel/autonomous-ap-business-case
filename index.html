Autonomous AP Business Case & Cash-Risk Check

HSB, Sweden's largest housing cooperative, processes about 1.5 million mostly non-PO invoices a year. In year one of autonomous AP it reported 96% coding accuracy, 72% of invoices with no human touch and 60,000+ hours saved. Use this page to size the time you could get back at your own volume, set it against the cash you could put at risk, and check the six controls that keep that risk small.
